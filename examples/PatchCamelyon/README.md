# Patch Camelyon Medical Imaging

The Patch Camelyon dataset includes over 300,00 medical images which may or may not include a tumor.

## MLP

Due to the simple output classification, even a simple MLP can perform relatively well on the Patch Camelyon dataset.

## CNN

Unsurprisingly, a CNN performs quite well on the dataset sue to the input being relatively complex images, ideal for CNNs to use.
